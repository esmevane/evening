Devotions are powerful specializations or knacks that distinguish you from the rest of your peers. Devotions are part of the [[rulebooks/abandoned-wip-reference/evening-core-v1/Progression|progression]] of characters which reflects specializations in your chosen classes, or focus on a new kind of development entirely.

**At first level.** At third level, you gain one devotion level. You also gain the devotion kit of the devotion you select.

**At higher levels.** As you progress, you gain additional focus levels at 7th, 11th, 15th, and 19th character level. You can spend those to increase the level of your current devotion or you can choose to purchase a new devotion which you are qualified for.

**Multi-devotion.** You may pick to advance any devotion you like whenever you spend a devotion level.

**Re-designing your devotion.** Whenever you would gain a devotion level, you may exchange one devotion level that you have already spent for a different devotion that you qualify for. You have learned something new and chosen to put aside something you no longer wish to do.
